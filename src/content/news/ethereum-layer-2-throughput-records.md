The aggregate scaling capacity of the **Ethereum (ETH)** network reached a milestone this week as total throughput across Layer-2 rollups exceeded 2,500 transactions per second (TPS). The metric represents a 400% expansion year-over-year, confirming Ethereum’s modular scaling roadmap has achieved production-grade performance.

Data aggregated by L2BEAT indicates that layer-2 ecosystems now settle more than 92% of all Ethereum-denominated transaction activity, while Layer-1 serves primarily as a decentralized settlement and consensus root of trust.

## The Economic Impact of Blobs and Proto-Danksharding

Following the implementation of EIP-4844 (Proto-Danksharding), transaction costs on rollups fell by over 90%. Users on networks like Base, Arbitrum One, and Scroll routinely pay less than half a cent for decentralized token swaps.

Ethereum mainnet has simultaneously maintained gas prices between 8 and 14 Gwei, ensuring that base-layer transactions such as large-value multi-signature transfers, high-yield staking, and governance votes remain predictably priced.

## Preparing for the Pectra Hard Fork

Core protocol engineers are finalizing testnet deployments for Ethereum’s next major milestone: the **Pectra** network upgrade (combining Prague and Electra). 

Pectra introduces several critical Ethereum Improvement Proposals (EIPs):

* **EIP-7702 (Account Abstraction):** Spearheaded by Vitalik Buterin, this proposal allows standard wallets (EOAs) to temporarily act like smart contract wallets during transaction execution. Users will gain one-click approvals, batch token transfers, and the ability for dApps to sponsor transaction gas costs entirely.
* **EIP-7251 (MaxEB - Increase Max Effective Balance):** Raises the maximum validator balance from 32 ETH to 2,048 ETH, allowing large institutional staking entities to consolidate operations, reducing peer-to-peer gossip network load.
* **Blob Capacity Adjustments:** Enhances data availability bandwidth to accommodate high-throughput rollup sequencers.

## Economic Security and Staking Participation

The total amount of staked Ether has surpassed **34.2 million ETH**, representing over 28.5% of total circulating supply. This economic security ensures that conducting a re-organization or double-spend attack on Ethereum would require mobilizing over \$115 billion in capital, making it by far the most economically fortified smart contract platform in existence.

As institutional asset managers launch staked ETH exchange-traded products internationally, staking yields (currently fluctuating between 3.1% and 3.6% APR) continue to serve as the risk-free reference benchmark for decentralized finance yields.
